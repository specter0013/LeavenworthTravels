Christmas Lighting Festival

Three December weekends when Leavenworth glows — carolers, chestnuts, sleigh rides, and the lighting ceremony at dusk.

Festival dates

2026 season (placeholder): the first three weekends of December — Dec 5–6, Dec 12–13, and Dec 19–20. The town lights up at dusk each festival day.

A festival day, hour by hour

  1. Afternoon

    Stroll Front Street: roasted chestnuts, carolers, and holiday shops open late.

  2. Dusk (~4:30 pm)

    The lighting ceremony — thousands of lights come on at once, with carols led from the gazebo stage.

  3. Evening

    Sleigh rides, visits with Santa, and dinner downtown — book tables ahead on festival weekends.

Festival-weekend tips

  • Arrive early. Parking fills by mid-afternoon; the shuttle from the fairgrounds is the easiest option.
  • Dress for snow. It's an outdoor town — warm layers, waterproof boots, and hand warmers.
  • Book lodging months ahead. December weekends sell out early — see Places to Stay.
  • Eat on the early side. Front Street restaurants fill fast after the lighting — see Places to Eat.
