Stevens Pass

The closest big ski mountain to Leavenworth — a scenic drive over US-2, with terrain for every level.

Quick facts

~45 min drive from Leavenworth over US-2
1,900+ acres of skiable terrain
Dec–Apr typical season
All levels beginner to expert

On the mountain

Stevens Pass sits on US-2 between Leavenworth and Seattle, with a mix of groomed cruisers, tree runs, and backside terrain. Lift tickets, rentals, and lessons are all available at the base area — beginners should book lessons ahead for holiday weekends.

Official Stevens Pass site

Getting there

  • The drive: roughly 45 minutes from Leavenworth over US-2 through Tumwater Canyon — scenic, but watch for ice.
  • Check conditions first: look up WSDOT pass reports before you go, and carry chains in winter.
  • Leave early: weekend traffic builds from both sides of the pass by mid-morning.

Make it a full trip

Warm up after with dinner in town — see Places to Eat — and stay overnight instead of driving back in the dark: see Places to Stay.
